I'm a DevOps engineer who works across the entire software lifecycle — from interfaces and APIs down to the infrastructure and pipelines underneath them.

On the platform side I work with Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Terraform, Ansible, Linux and AWS. Being an RHCSA-certified Linux administrator means I'm comfortable where most problems actually live — the OS. On the product side I build with React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, MongoDB and SQL.

Day to day I keep production healthy: monitoring live infrastructure, building Grafana dashboards on ClickHouse data, writing Python automation for operational workflows and digging into performance when things get slow.

300+ LeetCode problems in C++ — including the full Top Interview 150 and Top SQL 50 — keep my data-structures and performance instincts sharp.

In simple words: I make sure apps and websites reach people safely — and stay fast and online after they do.

When developers write new code, I've built the “conveyor belt” that automatically checks it, packages it and puts it live. No late-night manual uploads, no “but it worked on my laptop”.

Once it's live, I watch over it. My dashboards show how healthy every server is, and alerts tell me when something looks wrong — so it gets fixed before customers notice.

I can also build apps myself (websites with React and Node.js), and I've solved 300+ coding puzzles on LeetCode to keep my problem-solving sharp.
